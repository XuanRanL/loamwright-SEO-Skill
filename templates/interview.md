# Interview Template

> Format ID: `interview`. Q&A with expert / leader. Strong E-E-A-T + backlink magnet.

## Default structure

```
{Subject Name}, {Title at Company}: {Specific Topic}

## TL;DR (40-60w)
Who the interviewee is + 2 most surprising takeaways.

## Introduction (~250-300w — who is this person)
- Background + credentials
- Why they're qualified to speak on this
- Why we wanted to interview them
- real first-hand experience (plain prose) (your relationship to interviewee)

## The conversation (8-12 Q&A pairs)

**Q: {Specific question — not generic}**

A: [interviewee response, ≤200w each]

## Key takeaways (~200-300w)
Synthesized lessons from the interview.

## What this means for {audience} (~250-300w)
Application of insights.

## More from {Subject} (~150-200w)
Their work / book / social links.

## Methodology (~100w — E-E-A-T)
- When the interview happened
- Format (video call / in-person / async written)
- Edited for clarity (or not)

## References (≤8 — including interviewee's published work)
```

## Word budget (3500-4500w)

| Section | Words | % |
|---|---|---|
| TL;DR + intro | 350 | 9 |
| 10 Q&A × 250w | 2500 | 64 |
| Key takeaways | 250 | 6 |
| What this means | 275 | 7 |
| More + methodology | 250 | 6 |
| Hooks + transitions | 275 | 7 |

## Required modifiers
- `tldr-first`, `citation-capsules-per-h2`, `mandatory-toc`
- `strong-eeat-signals` ✓ — expert authority transfer
- `info-gain-prose` ✓ ≥1 piece of real first-hand experience

## Hard rules
1. Real interview (don't make up Q&A — T04 veto adjacent)
2. Interviewee's credentials prominent
3. Questions are SPECIFIC (not "tell me about your work")
4. Answers preserved (not paraphrased into authorial voice)
5. Photo / image of interviewee (E-E-A-T signal)
6. Disclosure: any conflict of interest (paid sponsor, friendship, etc.)

## Schema additions
- `Article` base
- `Person` for INTERVIEWEE (not just author)
- `Person` for author
- `Organization` for publisher
- `Interview` schema (newer; use if Google supports for your vertical)

## Image slots
- Cover: photo of interviewee
- Section 1: interviewee + author together (if in-person)
- Section 2: relevant artifact (book cover, product, lab, etc.)

## Backlink potential

Interviews are TOP backlink magnets:
- Interviewee shares to their network → backlinks + traffic
- Their employer might link
- Their books / products link back
- Industry trades reference

ROI on interviews: 10x compared to standard articles (in backlinks + brand authority).

## When to use vs case-study vs personal-story
- case-study: data + outcome focused
- interview: opinion + experience of OTHER expert
- personal-story: opinion + experience of AUTHOR

## Common pitfalls
- ❌ Generic questions ("What do you think about the future?")
- ❌ Yes/no questions (lose nuance)
- ❌ Paraphrasing answers (lose voice; cite directly)
- ❌ Missing interviewee bio
- ❌ No photo of subject
- ❌ Hidden sponsorship / friendship disclosure
- ❌ Questions all friendly (no real challenge)

## Tip: ask one hard question

Best interviews include at least one provocative question. Builds reader trust and shows journalistic integrity.
