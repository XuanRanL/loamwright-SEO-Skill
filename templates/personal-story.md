# Personal Story Template

> Format ID: `personal-story`. First-hand narrative with universal lessons.
> Best for: founder stories, "I tried X", career pivots, mistakes-learned.

## Default structure

```
{Specific Title — NOT "How I..."}: {Concrete Outcome}

## TL;DR (40-60w)
What I did + what I learned + what you can take from it.

## The setup (~300-400w)
Where I was, what I was trying to do.
Specific: dates, places, dollar amounts.

## The decision / event (~400-500w)
What happened. Concrete details.
real first-hand experience (plain prose). Sensory detail.

## What I tried (~500-700w)
The actual attempt(s). Honest about iteration.
What worked first attempt vs what failed.

## What surprised me (~300-400w)
Counter-intuitive insights. This is the unique-value section.
original analysis (plain prose).

## What I'd do differently (~300-400w)
Honest hindsight. Not self-flagellation; analytical.

## What this means for you (~300-400w)
Translate the experience to reader's context.
"If you're in X situation, consider Y."

## What I won't tell you yet (~150-200w, optional)
Acknowledge what's still unfolding / private.

## References (~6 — your own data + 2-3 supporting)
```

## Word budget (3500w target)

| Section | Words | % |
|---|---|---|
| TL;DR + setup | 400 | 11 |
| Decision/event | 450 | 13 |
| What I tried | 600 | 17 |
| What surprised me | 350 | 10 |
| Hindsight | 350 | 10 |
| For reader | 350 | 10 |
| Unresolved | 175 | 5 |
| Transitions / hooks | 825 | 24 |

## Required modifiers
- `tldr-first`, `citation-capsules-per-h2` (still applies — citation per H2)
- `info-gain-prose` ✓ REQUIRES real first-hand experience + original analysis, both in plain prose
- `strong-eeat-signals` ✓ — experience IS the value

## Hard rules
1. ≥1 piece(s) of real first-hand experience
2. ≥1 piece of original analysis
3. SPECIFIC details: dates, dollar amounts, names (where allowed)
4. Sensory detail (smell of coffee at 2am, the feel of the keyboard)
5. NO universal "we all" framing (that's essay; this is YOUR story)
6. End with what reader can do (NOT just "thanks for reading")

## Schema additions
- `Article` (or `BlogPosting`)
- `Person` author with detailed bio
- `Organization`

## Voice + purpose
- Voice: `casual` or `warm` (not `professional` — too distant)
- Purpose: `general` or `essay`

## What this format is NOT
- ❌ Pure storytelling without lessons (no SEO value)
- ❌ Marketing case study (use case-study template for that)
- ❌ Opinion piece (use opinion template — opinion is claim-driven)
- ❌ Generic "career advice" framed as personal (be specific to YOUR story)

## What this format IS
- ✓ Specific experiences with universal takeaways
- ✓ Honest about failures
- ✓ Counter-intuitive insights (not "work hard" — too obvious)
- ✓ Reader's-perspective takeaway

## Image slots
- Cover: photo from the actual experience (NOT stock)
- Section 1: artifact (e.g., screenshot of the decision spreadsheet)
- Section 2: result / outcome image
- Optional: author headshot for E-E-A-T

## Tone

First-person ("I", "me") natural throughout. Use "you" only in the "what this means for you" section.

## Common pitfalls
- ❌ Generic ("I learned hard work pays off")
- ❌ Self-aggrandizing
- ❌ Naming-drop without substance
- ❌ All-positive (no failures = not believable)
- ❌ Forgetting the reader takeaway
- ❌ Stock photography for cover
