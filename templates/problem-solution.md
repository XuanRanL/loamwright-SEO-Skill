# Problem-Solution Template

> Format ID: `problem-solution`. Address a specific reader problem with actionable solution.

## Default structure

```
{Specific Problem}: {Action Verb} Solution

## TL;DR (40-60w)
The fix: do X.

## The problem (~300-400w)
- Symptoms
- Common causes
- Why DIY usually fails

## Why other solutions fall short (~300w)
Top 3-5 "wrong fixes" + why they don't work.

## The solution (~600-800w step-by-step)
Step 1: ...
Step 2: ...
Step 3: ...
Photos / examples per step.

## Variations / edge cases (~300w)
"If your situation is X, do Y instead."

## What to do if this doesn't work (~250w)
Next-level options.

## FAQ (5-7)

## References (≤8)
```

## Word budget (2500w)

## Required modifiers
- `tldr-first`, `citation-capsules-per-h2`, `featured-snippet-targets`, `info-gain-prose` ≥1

## Hard rules
1. Lead with concrete problem (NOT "in today's world")
2. Acknowledge what doesn't work + why
3. ≥1 piece(s) of real first-hand experience
4. Steps numbered, each ≤80 words

## When to use vs how-to-guide
- how-to: neutral instruction ("how to set up X")
- problem-solution: starts with pain point ("X isn't working — here's how to fix it")

## Schema additions
- `Article` + `HowTo` as `mainEntity` (NEVER HowTo as primary @type — T09 veto)
