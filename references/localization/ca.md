## Bilingual consideration

- 22% of Canadians are French-first speakers (mostly Quebec)
- Federal regulations: French equivalents required for federal businesses
- **Quebec Bill 96** (2022): strict French language requirements for ANY business in Quebec
- For Canada-wide SEO: en-CA covers ~78% of population; add fr-CA for Quebec market

If targeting Quebec, use `references/localization/fr.md` for the French version.

## Market characteristics

- High AI / tech adoption (Toronto = major AI hub)
- Strong US influence + cultural distinctiveness
- 60% mobile traffic
- Privacy-conscious (PIPEDA + provincial variants)

## Cultural references

- Sports: NHL (hockey is THE sport), CFL football, curling
- Holidays: Canada Day (Jul 1), Thanksgiving (2nd Monday Oct), Boxing Day (Dec 26)
- Tim Hortons / Loblaws / Canadian Tire as cultural anchors
- Politeness as cultural value ("sorry" as filler)
- Hockey + outdoor + multicultural themes resonate

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.ca dominant (~91%)
- "[X] Canada" suffix common
- Mobile-first
- AI search rising fast

## Search behavior

## Province-specific notes

- **Ontario**: most populous; English dominant; tech / finance hub (Toronto)
- **Quebec**: French dominant; Bill 96 strict; cultural distinctiveness
- **British Columbia**: tech (Vancouver); Pacific-facing
- **Alberta**: energy industry; conservative cultural lean
- **Atlantic provinces**: smaller markets; tourism-heavy
- **Prairies (MB/SK)**: agricultural; smaller online markets

For nationwide content: target Ontario + BC primarily; add Quebec via fr-CA.
