# Locale: United Kingdom (en-UK / en-GB)

UK market context for SEO content.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Spelling | en-UK (see `spelling/en-UK.md`) |
| Currency | GBP `£` |
| Date format | `19 May 2026` or `19/05/2026` |
| Distance | miles + km (mixed); kilometres in scientific |
| Weight | pounds + kilograms; "stone" for body weight |
| Temperature | °C |
| Default voice | restrained, understated, self-deprecating humour |
| Tone | formal-leaning; "Hi" not "Hey"; less direct than US |

## Market characteristics

- Sophisticated SEO market with high E-E-A-T expectations
- B2C: less aggressive than US; focus on quality + heritage
- B2B: relationship-driven; longer sales cycles
- Mobile traffic ~55-60%
- AI tool adoption strong (especially ChatGPT)

## Cultural references that resonate

- Sports: Premier League football (NOT soccer), cricket, rugby, F1
- Holidays: Boxing Day (Dec 26), Bank Holidays (May, Aug), Bonfire Night (Nov 5)
- Royalty: minimize unless industry-relevant
- Class consciousness: subtle; affects tone calibration
- BBC + Guardian reading levels common reference points

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.co.uk dominant (~93% market share)
- Bing share slightly higher than US
- AI search rising
- ASA strict on advertising claims (more than FTC)
- Schema.org expected; Yoast / Rank Math common

## Search behavior

- "Best [X] UK" suffix common (location filtering)
- Cookery → "recipe" terms (NOT "cooking" alone)
- Voice search via Google Assistant + Siri growing
- Price-conscious queries common ("cheap", "budget", "value")

## Regulatory + compliance

- ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) — STRICT on claims
- MHRA (Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency) — health
- Ofcom — telecom + broadcasting
- FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) — financial
- CMA (Competition and Markets Authority) — competition law
- ICO — data protection (GDPR + UK GDPR)

**Affiliate disclosure rule**: must be CLEAR and PROMINENT (ASA stricter than FTC). Standard: "This article contains affiliate links. We may earn commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you. This does not affect our editorial recommendations."

## Cookie consent

UK GDPR + PECR require:
- Cookie consent banner BEFORE non-essential cookies
- Granular control (not all-or-nothing)
- Easy withdrawal

If publishing in UK: ensure cookie consent is in place on the site.

## YMYL caution

Same as US (health/financial/legal/safety) BUT:
- ASA more aggressive on health claims
- Financial promotions must comply with FCA
- "Investment" claims need disclaimers + risk warnings

## Content length norms

Similar to US, slightly shorter on average:

| Content type | Typical word count |
|---|---|
| News-analysis | 600-1,200 |
| How-to guide | 1,500-3,500 |
| Listicle | 3,000-5,500 |
| Pillar page | 4,000-7,000 |

UK readers prefer concise + dense over US "stretch to reach 6,000 words".

## Idioms (UK-natural)

See `references/localization/spelling/en-UK.md` for full list.

Common avoidances:
- ❌ "Awesome" / "Amazing" (over-the-top to UK ears)
- ❌ "You guys" (US-specific)
- ❌ "Holler at me" (very US)
- ✓ "Brilliant" / "Excellent" (positive without over-the-top)
- ✓ "Cheers" as sign-off
- ✓ "Got in touch" instead of "reached out"

## Time zones

- GMT (UTC+0) — winter
- BST (UTC+1) — summer (March - October)

Publish at 8 AM GMT for UK business hours.
