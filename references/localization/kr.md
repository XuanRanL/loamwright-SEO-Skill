# Locale: Korea (ko / kr)

Korean market context.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Currency | KRW `₩` or `원` (`₩1,234` — usually no decimals; comma thousands) |
| Date format | `2026년 5월 19일` or `2026.05.19` |
| Distance | km, m (metric only) |
| Weight | kg, g (metric only) |
| Temperature | °C |
| Voice | 합쇼체 / 해요체 (formal speech levels) by default |
| Word count | 25-35% shorter than English |

## Language notes

- **Hangul (한글)**: the only writing system for modern Korean. Avoid mixing Hanja (Chinese characters) unless audience is academic.
- **Speech levels**:
  - 합쇼체 (hapsho-che): highest formal, B2B + government
  - 해요체 (haeyo-che): polite, default for most content
  - 해체 (hae-che): casual, B2C youth (use sparingly)
- **No spaces in compound nouns**: but spaces between words.
- **Particles**: -은/는 (topic), -이/가 (subject), -을/를 (object), -에 (location), etc. Important for meaning.
- **Quotation marks**: " " (Western style) for direct quotes.

## Market characteristics

- Highest mobile adoption globally (~80% traffic)
- Tech-savvy audience; early adopters of new platforms
- Naver dominant (60-70%) vs Google (~25%)
- KakaoTalk + KakaoStory ecosystems
- Strong K-content global influence
- B2C: visual + influencer-driven
- B2B: relationship + hierarchy-aware

## Cultural references

- Holidays: Lunar New Year (Seollal), Chuseok (Korean Thanksgiving, Sept/Oct), Hangul Day (Oct 9), Christmas (commercial)
- K-pop, K-drama, K-beauty as common touchpoints
- Education + hagwon (cram school) culture
- Generational differences explicit (Gen Z = MZ세대)

## SEO + GEO specifics

- **Naver dominant** in Korea (~60-70%) — different SEO from Google
- Google.co.kr also strong (~25-30%)
- Daum/Kakao smaller share
- Naver SEO: Naver blog posts ranked highest in Naver; Cafe (community forum) content important
- Mobile-first; AMP / Naver mobile cards matter
- Google.co.kr behaves like other Google markets

## Search behavior

- Voice search growing (NaverClova)
- "추천" (recommendation), "비교" (comparison), "후기" (review) common
- Very long-tail with specific brand names
- KakaoTalk-style abbreviations leak into queries

## Regulatory + compliance

- PIPC — Personal Information Protection Commission (similar to GDPR)
- KCC — Korea Communications Commission (telecom + ads)
- MFDS — Food and Drug Safety
- FSC — Financial Services Commission

**Affiliate disclosure**: "*이 글은 제휴 링크를 포함하고 있습니다. 링크를 통해 구매하시면 추가 비용 없이 저희가 소정의 수수료를 받게 됩니다.*"

## YMYL caution

- Health: MFDS strict on medical/supplement claims
- Financial: FSC requires warnings + disclaimers
- Legal: 변호사 (byeonhosa, licensed lawyers) only

## Content length norms

| Content type | English (words) | Korean (characters 글자) |
|---|---|---|
| News-analysis | 1,000w | 2,500-3,000 글자 |
| How-to guide | 2,500w | 6,000-7,500 글자 |
| Listicle | 5,000w | 12,000-15,000 글자 |
| Pillar page | 6,000w | 15,000-18,000 글자 |

## Tone calibration

- Respectful + hierarchical-aware (use 님 honorific in B2B)
- Direct but not aggressive
- Group / "우리" (we, our) framing common
- Younger audiences (MZ세대): more casual + Konglish words
- Use emoji moderately in B2C (heavy use in K-blogging style)

## Distinctive features

- **Konglish**: English loanwords used Korean-style (e.g., 핸드폰 hand phone = mobile)
- **Naver-specific SEO**: optimize for Naver blog + Cafe + Knowledge iN
- **Visual content**: image-heavy posts perform better
- **Hashtags**: less central than US; categories matter more
