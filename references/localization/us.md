## Market characteristics

- World's largest English-speaking market by GDP
- B2C: split by region (East coast more formal, West coast more casual, South more relationship-oriented)
- B2B: Slack-style direct communication dominant in tech; more formal in finance / law
- Mobile-first: 60%+ traffic from mobile for most B2C
- AI tool adoption: highest globally; ChatGPT + Claude + Perplexity all heavily used

## Cultural references that resonate

- Sports: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football
- Holidays: Thanksgiving (3rd Thursday Nov), 4th of July, Memorial Day, Labor Day
- Pop culture: TV shows widely shared (Netflix, HBO, Disney+)
- Politics: avoid unless directly relevant
- Regional differences are real (Texas ≠ New York ≠ California)

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google search dominant (~92% market share)
- AI search rising fast (ChatGPT search 5% and growing)
- Voice search via Google Assistant + Siri high
- Featured snippets + AIO heavily contested
- Schema.org markup expected (Yoast / Rank Math standard)

## Search behavior

- Question-based queries common ("how to", "what is", "best")
- Voice queries trending up
- Local search heavy ("near me", "in [city]")
- Mobile + local intersect in "near me" queries

## Regulatory + compliance

- FTC (Federal Trade Commission) — affiliate disclosure
- FDA — health / drug claims (YMYL)
- FCC — telecom
- SEC — financial claims
- COPPA — children's content (under 13)
- ADA — accessibility (alt text, contrast)

Affiliate disclosure rule: "Conspicuous and clear" disclosure must precede or accompany affiliate link. Standard text: "As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases."

## YMYL caution

US content on these topics requires E-E-A-T credentials:
- Health/medical advice
- Financial advice (investment, tax, loans)
- Legal advice
- Safety topics
- Election / civic info

Author Person schema must include credentials (license, degree, professional title) for YMYL or T05 veto fires.

## Content length norms

| Content type | Typical word count |
|---|---|
| News-analysis | 800-1,500 |
| How-to guide | 2,000-4,000 |
| Listicle (top 10) | 4,000-6,000 |
| Pillar page | 4,500-8,000 |
| Case study | 3,000-5,000 |

US readers tolerate longer content for high-value topics; shorter for utilitarian.
