# Locale: Germany (de)

German market context. Single-largest EU economy.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Currency | EUR `€` (after number: `1.234,56 €`) |
| Date format | `19. Mai 2026` or `19.05.2026` (DD.MM.YYYY) |
| Distance | km, metres (metric only) |
| Weight | kg, grams (metric only) |
| Temperature | °C |
| Default voice | direct, precise, formal "Sie" by default |
| Word count | 20-30% LONGER than English (German is verbose) |

## Language notes

**Critical**: German content is NOT just English translation.

- **Sie vs du**: B2B + most marketing = "Sie" (formal). B2C casual brands = "du" (informal). Pick ONE per article + stick with it.
- **Compound nouns**: German loves compound nouns (Marketingautomatisierungssoftware). Don't break them up.
- **Capitalization**: All nouns capitalized (Marketing, Software, Lösung).
- **Word order**: Verb-second in main clauses, verb-final in subordinate clauses. AI translations often get this wrong.

## Market characteristics

- Strict privacy expectations (GDPR + native regulation)
- B2B engineering culture (technical detail expected)
- Skeptical of marketing claims (less hype, more proof)
- Long sales cycles (60-90 days typical)
- High SEO sophistication

## Cultural references

- Football (NOT soccer): Bundesliga, national team
- Holidays: Christmas markets (Dec), Oktoberfest (regional, Sept-Oct), Tag der Deutschen Einheit (Oct 3)
- Engineering excellence as cultural value
- Sustainability + green tech resonate

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.de dominant (~93%)
- Ecosia (eco-search engine) has 1-2% share
- Strong demand for technical depth
- E-E-A-T critical (German market expects credentials)

## Search behavior

- Long, specific queries common ("Vergleich" = comparison searches)
- Less voice search than English markets
- Mobile traffic ~55%
- "Test" / "Vergleich" / "Erfahrung" common modifiers

## Regulatory + compliance

- DSGVO (GDPR) — strictest in EU enforcement
- TKG (Telekommunikationsgesetz) — telecom
- TMG (Telemediengesetz) — media regulation
- UWG (Gesetz gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb) — advertising
- BMG (Bundesministerium für Gesundheit) — health claims
- BaFin — financial advice

**Affiliate disclosure**: "*Dieser Artikel enthält Affiliate-Links. Bei einem Kauf über diese Links erhalten wir eine Provision, ohne dass für dich zusätzliche Kosten entstehen.*" (or formal "Sie" variant)

Position: top of article OR right before each link. NOT just footer.

**Imprint (Impressum)**: legally required on every page. Standard German web compliance.

## YMYL caution

- Health: HWG (Heilmittelwerbegesetz) restricts health advertising
- Financial: WpHG (Wertpapierhandelsgesetz) regulates investment claims
- Legal: only lawyers (Rechtsanwalt) can give legal advice

## Content length norms

German typically 20-30% longer than English equivalent:

| Content type | English | German |
|---|---|---|
| News-analysis | 1,000w | 1,300-1,500w |
| How-to guide | 2,500w | 3,000-3,500w |
| Listicle | 5,000w | 6,500-7,500w |
| Pillar page | 6,000w | 7,500-9,000w |

## Tone calibration

- More formal than English (Sie default)
- More precise (less metaphor, more data)
- Less superlative ("ausgezeichnet" = excellent, but used sparingly)
- "Bitte" (please) standard
- Humour: dry, understated; avoid US-style enthusiasm

## Distinctive phrases

| English | German equivalent |
|---|---|
| "In this article, we will explore..." | "Dieser Artikel zeigt..." (avoid filler) |
| "Best of the best" | "Die besten" (skip "of the best") |
| "Cutting-edge" | "Modernst" or just describe specifically (avoid hype) |
| "Easy to use" | "Benutzerfreundlich" or "einfach in der Anwendung" |
| "Game-changer" | (no direct equivalent; describe specifically) |

## Time zones

- CET (UTC+1) — winter
- CEST (UTC+2) — summer (late March - late October)

Publish at 8 AM CET.

## Banned in de marketing (UWG enforcement)

- "Beste" (best) without proof
- "Größte" (largest) without verifiable claim
- "Marktführer" (market leader) without data
- "Garantiert" (guaranteed) for non-warrantied claims

UWG fines for unsupported superlatives are real.
