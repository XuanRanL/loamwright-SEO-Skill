# Locale: Netherlands (nl) + Belgium-Dutch (nl-BE)

Dutch market context. Small population, high digital sophistication.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Currency | EUR `€` (before number: `€ 1.234,56`) |
| Date format | `19 mei 2026` (lowercase month) or `19-05-2026` |
| Decimal | `1.234,56` (dot thousands, comma decimal) |

| Voice | "u" formal / "je" informal |

## Language notes

- **U vs je**: B2B + formal marketing = "u". Casual B2C = "je". Younger audiences (under 35): "je" widely accepted.
- **Word order**: V2 in main, verb-final in subordinate (similar to German).
- **English loanwords**: Dutch readily absorbs English in tech ("computer", "software", "online" all OK).
- **Capitalization**: nouns NOT capitalized (unlike German).
- **Diaeresis**: keep "ë", "ï", "ü" — important for correct spelling.

## Variant: nl-BE (Flanders, Belgium)

- Slightly more formal than Netherlands Dutch
- Different vocabulary (e.g., "GSM" for phone vs "mobiel")
- "Frietjes" (Belgium) vs "patat" (Netherlands) — fries

For SEO: target nl-NL primarily; add nl-BE if specifically targeting Belgium.

## Market characteristics

- Highly digital + English-fluent (90%+ speak English)
- Strong B2B SaaS market (Amsterdam = European tech hub)
- E-commerce sophisticated
- Privacy-conscious (GDPR enforcement)
- Direct communication culture
