# Locale: Spain + Spanish (es / es-MX / es-AR / es-CO)

Spanish market context. **Important: Spain Spanish ≠ Latin American Spanish.**

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Spain (es-ES) | Mexico (es-MX) | Argentina (es-AR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Currency | EUR `€` | MXN `$` (or `MX$`) | ARS `$` (or `AR$`) |
| Date format | `19 de mayo de 2026` or `19/05/2026` | Same (sometimes US `5/19`) | Same as Spain |
| Decimal | `1.234,56` | `1,234.56` (US-style) | `1.234,56` |
| Distance | km | km (often miles in border areas) | km |
| Temperature | °C | °C | °C |
| Vous-tu equivalent | "usted" formal, "tú" informal | "usted"/"tú" | "vos" (Argentina-specific) |

**For SEO targeting**: pick ONE variant per article. Don't mix Spain + Mexico Spanish.

## Quick rule for choosing variant

- Spain (es-ES): Europe-only audience or general "Spanish from Spain" feel
- Mexico (es-MX): North America + Central America audience; "neutral" Latin American
- Latin American "neutral" (es-419): pan-LATAM, avoids regionalisms
- Argentina (es-AR): specifically Argentina (uses voseo "vos" instead of "tú")

## Market characteristics

- Spain: similar to Germany — sophisticated SEO market, GDPR enforcement
- Mexico: largest Spanish-speaking market by economy + population
- Argentina: smaller but highly literate; strong tech / SaaS adoption
- Lifestyle / food / travel content high engagement across all

## Cultural references

- **Spain**: football (La Liga, FC Barcelona, Real Madrid), siestas, tapas, regional identities (Catalonia, Basque)
- **Mexico**: football (Liga MX), wrestling (Lucha Libre), Día de los Muertos, family-centered
- **Argentina**: football (Boca, River), tango, mate (beverage), Italian-influenced culture

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google dominant across all Spanish-speaking markets (~93%)
- Mobile traffic 60-70% (higher in LATAM than Spain)
- AI search growing fast in Mexico

## Search behavior

- "Mejor" (best) for commercial
- "Cómo" (how) for informational
- "Cerca de mí" (near me) for local
- Voice search adoption growing

## Regulatory + compliance

**Spain (es-ES)**:
- AEPD — strict GDPR (matches Germany)
- DGOJ — gambling advertising
- AESAN — health/food

**Mexico (es-MX)**:
- PROFECO — consumer protection (advertising)
- COFEPRIS — health
- CNBV — financial

**Argentina (es-AR)**:
- AAIP — data protection
- ANMAT — health

**Affiliate disclosure (universal Spanish)**: "*Este artículo contiene enlaces de afiliados. Si compras a través de estos enlaces, podemos recibir una comisión sin costo adicional para ti.*"

(Adjust "ti" → "usted" for formal variant; "vos" for Argentine.)

## Content length norms

| Content type | English | Spanish (any variant) |
|---|---|---|
| News-analysis | 1,000w | 1,100-1,300w |
| How-to guide | 2,500w | 2,800-3,200w |
| Listicle | 5,000w | 5,500-6,500w |
| Pillar page | 6,000w | 6,800-8,000w |

Spanish ~10-20% longer than English equivalent.

## Tone calibration

- Warm + relationship-oriented (more than English)
- Honorifics matter (Don/Doña in formal)
- Family / community references resonate
- Less direct than US, more direct than German
- Humour: regionally varied (Argentina = irony; Mexico = wordplay; Spain = political)

## Time zones

- Spain: CET/CEST (UTC+1/+2)
- Mexico: 3 zones (CT, MT, PT analogous)
- Argentina: UTC-3 (year-round)
- Colombia: UTC-5
- Peru: UTC-5

Publish: 9 AM local for each major market.
