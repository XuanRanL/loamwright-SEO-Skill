# Locale: Taiwan (zh-TW, Traditional Chinese)

Taiwan market. **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)**. Different vocabulary from mainland China.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Currency | TWD `NT$` (`NT$1,234`) |
| Date format | `2026年5月19日` (民國115年5月19日 in official) or `2026/05/19` |
| Distance | km, m (metric only) |
| Weight | kg, g; sometimes 斤 (catty) for food |
| Temperature | °C |
| Punctuation | Full-width (，。！？；：「」) |

## Language notes

- **Traditional characters (繁體)**: 國 not 国; 學 not 学; 龍 not 龙.
- **Vocabulary differs from mainland**:
  - 影片 (TW) vs 视频 (CN) = video
  - 軟體 (TW) vs 软件 (CN) = software
  - 行動 (TW) vs 移动 (CN) = mobile
  - 電郵/電子郵件 (TW) vs 邮箱/邮件 (CN) = email
  - 計程車 (TW) vs 出租车 (CN) = taxi
- **Tone**: somewhat more polite than mainland
- **Punctuation**: full-width but spacing more like English

## Market characteristics

- 23M people; high digital adoption
- **Google dominant** (~94%) — unlike mainland
- LINE (Japanese chat app) more popular than WeChat
- Mobile traffic ~70%
- Strong democratic culture + open internet

## Cultural references

- Holidays: Lunar New Year (春節), Mid-Autumn (中秋), Tomb-Sweeping (清明), Double Tenth (Oct 10 — National Day)
- Night market culture (夜市)
- Bubble tea (珍珠奶茶) as cultural export
- LINE stickers + chat culture
- Karaoke (KTV) social

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.com.tw dominant
- Yahoo Taiwan still has ~5% share
- Mobile-first
- AI search adoption growing
- Traditional Chinese content lower competition than Simplified (smaller market = easier rankings)

## Tone calibration

- More polite than mainland China (similar to Japan)
- "您" (formal) used more freely
- Less direct than English; more direct than Japanese
- Younger audiences: more English mixing + LINE-style emoji

## Distinctive features

- **Bopomofo (ㄅㄆㄇㄈ)**: Taiwan's phonetic system (not Pinyin like mainland)
- **No traditional/simplified mixing**: stick with Traditional throughout
- **PTT (批踢踢)**: BBS-style social network unique to Taiwan
- **Boba/bubble tea references**: cultural shorthand

## Vocabulary differences vs mainland China

| Concept | Taiwan (繁體) | Mainland (简体) |
|---|---|---|
| Bus | 公車 | 公交车 |
| Taxi | 計程車 | 出租车 |
| Subway | 捷運 | 地铁 |
| Network | 網路 | 网络 |
| Information | 資訊 | 信息 |
| Database | 資料庫 | 数据库 |
| Server | 伺服器 | 服务器 |
| OK | 好 / 沒問題 | 行 / 没问题 |
