## Market characteristics

- Small population (~5.3M) but high digital adoption
- Mobile traffic ~62%
- Trading with both Asia (China #1 trading partner) + traditional Anglo allies
- Tourism + agriculture (dairy, wine) major sectors
- Smaller test market for SaaS / new products

## Cultural references

- Sports: rugby (All Blacks = national religion), netball, cricket
- Holidays: Waitangi Day (Feb 6), ANZAC Day (Apr 25), Matariki (Māori new year, June/July)
- Outdoors + adventure tourism
- Māori culture integrated (use respectfully; see spelling/en-NZ.md)
- LOTR / Hobbit filming locations (genuine national pride)

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.co.nz dominant (~94%)
- "[X] NZ" or "[X] New Zealand" common search modifier
- Mobile-first
- Smaller market = less competition → easier rankings for niche topics

## Search behavior

- Travel queries year-round (high inbound tourism)
- Sport queries spike during All Blacks tours + Six Nations
- Price comparison heavy ("vs Australia" common)

## Regulatory + compliance

- Commerce Commission — advertising + disclosure
- Medsafe — health/medical
- FMA (Financial Markets Authority) — financial
- Privacy Act (more strict than Australia)
- Fair Trading Act — consumer protection

## Māori cultural sensitivity

NZ Māori vocabulary is mainstream (kia ora, whānau, mana, etc.). Use accurately:

| Term | Use freely | Use with respect | Avoid in commercial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kia ora | greeting | — | — |
| Aotearoa | NZ poetic | — | — |
| Whānau | family extended | when contextually fitting | — |
| Mana | prestige/authority | when fitting | superficial branding use |
| Tapu | sacred | — | branding / commercial use |

Disrespectful Māori usage = brand damage in NZ.
