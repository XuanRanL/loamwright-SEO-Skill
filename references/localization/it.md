Italian market context.

## Language notes

- **Lei vs tu**: B2B + most marketing = "Lei" (formal). Casual B2C = "tu".
- **Articles**: Italian uses articles heavily (il, la, lo, gli, le). Don't drop them.
- **Pronouns**: subject often omitted ("vado" = I go, no "io" needed).
- **Quotes**: « » or " " both common; pick one + stick with it.

## Market characteristics

- Style + design-conscious (fashion, automotive, food)
- Family-centered + relationship-driven business
- Slow-but-loyal sales cycles
- Regional differences (Milan ≠ Rome ≠ Naples)
- Smaller SEO market than Germany / France but growing

## Cultural references

- Football: Serie A, national team (Azzurri)
- Holidays: Ferragosto (Aug 15), Festa della Repubblica (June 2), Christmas + Epiphany
- Food regionalism extreme (don't generalize "Italian food")
- Family Sunday lunch (la domenica)
- Coffee culture (cappuccino only before 11 AM; espresso always)

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.it dominant (~94%)
- Mobile traffic ~62%
- AI search adoption growing
- E-E-A-T expectations rising

## Search behavior

- "Migliore" (best) for commercial intent
- "Come" (how) for informational
- "Vicino a me" (near me) for local
- Long-tail with full natural phrasing

## Tone calibration

- Warm, expressive (more than English/German)
- Adjective-rich descriptions OK
- Family + tradition references resonate
- Less formal than German; more formal than US
- Humour: ironic + self-aware

## Distinctive features

- **Regionalism**: north/south differences in tone + vocabulary
  - Milan: business-formal
  - Rome: pragmatic + warm
  - Naples: expressive + colorful
- **Diminutives**: very common (caffè → caffettino, problema → problemino)
- **Quotation conventions**: «...» preferred but " " accepted

- CEST (UTC+2) — summer (late March - late October)
