# Locale: Australia (en-AU)

Australian market context.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Spelling | en-AU (follows UK; see `spelling/en-AU.md`) |
| Currency | AUD `AU$` or `$` when clear |
| Date format | `19 May 2026` or `19/05/2026` |
| Distance | km, metres (metric only) |

| Default voice | direct + friendly; "no worries" attitude |

## Market characteristics

- Highly digital; mobile ~65% of traffic
- Strong Asia-Pacific business orientation
- Lifestyle / outdoor content high engagement
- Sports (AFL, NRL, cricket) huge cultural anchor
- Coffee culture (sophisticated; "long black", "flat white")

## Cultural references

- Sports: AFL (Australian Football), NRL (Rugby League), cricket
- Holidays: Australia Day (Jan 26 — politically contested), ANZAC Day (Apr 25), Melbourne Cup (Nov)
- Slang: "mate" as universal term; "arvo", "brekkie", "servo" casual
- Beach + outdoor culture
- Underdog narrative resonates

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.com.au dominant (~94%)
- Strong "[X] Australia" or "[X] AU" search modifier
- Mobile-first indexing fully rolled out
- Local search heavy ("near me", "in [city]")

## Search behavior

- Price comparison heavy
- Sports + lifestyle content peaks during AFL/NRL seasons (Mar-Sep)
- Travel queries spike during EOFY (June) + summer (Dec-Feb)

## Regulatory + compliance

- ACCC (Australian Competition and Consumer Commission) — STRICTER than US FTC
- TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration) — health/medical
- ASIC (Australian Securities and Investments Commission) — financial
- ACMA — telecom + broadcasting
- Privacy Act — data protection

**Affiliate disclosure**: ACCC enforces strict transparency. Standard: "This article contains affiliate links. As an affiliate partner, we may earn from qualifying purchases. Our recommendations are independent."

**ACCC enforcement note**: avoid "Australia's best" / "leading" / "No. 1" unsupported superlatives — ACCC issues fines for misleading claims.

## YMYL caution

- Health claims: TGA approval needed for medicinal products
- Financial: AFSL (Australian Financial Services Licence) needed for advice
- Legal: cannot give legal advice without practicing certificate

## Distinctive vocabulary (when to use)

| Term | When OK | When NOT OK |
|---|---|---|
| "mate" | casual B2C | formal B2B |
| "arvo" / "brekkie" | lifestyle / casual | corporate / financial |
| "fair dinkum" | extremely casual | most content |
| "g'day" | greeting | NOT in formal opener |
| "no worries" | replacing "no problem" | acceptable in most contexts |

For corporate/finance audiences: stick to standard UK-style formal English. For lifestyle/B2C: AU slang adds authenticity.

## Time zones

- AEST (UTC+10) — Eastern (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane)
- ACST (UTC+9:30) — Central (Adelaide)
- AWST (UTC+8) — Western (Perth)
- Daylight saving: NSW, VIC, TAS, ACT, SA observe (Oct-Apr)

Publish at 8 AM AEST for max coverage.
