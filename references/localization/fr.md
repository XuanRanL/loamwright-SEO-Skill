## Language notes

- **Vous vs tu**: B2B + most marketing = "vous" (formal). Lifestyle / youth-targeted = "tu".
- **Espace insécable (non-breaking space)**: REQUIRED before `;`, `?`, `!`, `:`, after `«` and before `»`. WP / Word usually handle automatically.
- **Numbers**: comma decimal (`3,14`), space thousands (`1 234 567`) — French convention. NOT `3.14` or `1,234,567`.
- **Currency**: `1 234,56 €` (number first, space, symbol; comma decimal; space thousands).
- **Quotes**: « guillemets français » (with non-breaking space) — NOT English curly quotes.

## Market characteristics

- Strong identity around French language quality
- Académie française influences usage
- Less direct than US; more philosophical than UK
- Skeptical of Anglo marketing
- Strong B2C luxury market (Paris)

## Quebec specifics (fr-CA)

- Bill 96 — French required for ALL business in Quebec
- Different vocabulary from France French:
  - email = "courriel" (Quebec) vs "e-mail" (France)
  - parking = "stationnement" (Quebec) vs "parking" (France)
  - weekend = "fin de semaine" (Quebec) vs "weekend" (France)
- OQLF (Office québécois de la langue française) enforces standards

For Quebec content: use Quebec French specifically (not France French).

## Regulatory + compliance

- CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés) — strictest GDPR enforcement
- DGCCRF — competition + consumer protection (advertising)
- ANSM — health (medicines)
- AMF — financial markets
- ARCOM — telecom + media
- Loi Toubon — French language must dominate (no English-only content for B2C France)

## Tone calibration

- More elegant phrasing than direct US style
- Subordinate clauses + nuance valued
- Adjectives precede noun for emphasis ("magnifique château" vs "château magnifique")
- Less superlative; more measured praise
- Humour: dry wit, irony

## Time zones

- CET (UTC+1) — winter (France, Belgium, Luxembourg)
- CEST (UTC+2) — summer
- ET (UTC-5/-4) — Quebec
- CET (UTC+1) — Switzerland

Publish at 9 AM CET for France/Belgium/Switzerland; 9 AM ET for Quebec.
