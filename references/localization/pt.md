# Locale: Portuguese (pt-PT + pt-BR)

**Critical**: Portugal Portuguese (pt-PT) and Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) are DIFFERENT. Pick one per article.

## Quick variant comparison

| Concept | pt-PT (Portugal) | pt-BR (Brazil) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | telefone / telemóvel | telefone / celular |
| Bus | autocarro | ônibus |
| Train | comboio | trem |
| Toilet | casa de banho | banheiro |
| Computer | computador | computador (same) |
| OK | está bem | tá bom |
| Email | e-mail | e-mail (same) |
| 2nd-person you | você / tu | você (rarely "tu") |
| Past participle ending | -ado / -ido | -ado / -ido (same) |
| Word order | EU-influenced | more flexible |

| Dimension | Setting |

## Market characteristics

**Brazil (pt-BR)**:
- 210M people; massive market
- Mobile-first (~75% traffic)
- Strong WhatsApp ecosystem (business messaging)
- Less GDPR-strict than EU but LGPD (Brazilian GDPR) growing
- B2C dominant; B2B SaaS rising fast
- Football + carnival + family resonate

**Portugal (pt-PT)**:
- ~10M people
- EU-strict GDPR
- Strong tourism economy
- B2B more European-formal
- Smaller market but high digital adoption

## Cultural references

**Brazil**:
- Football (Brazil = world's best)
- Carnival, samba, bossa nova
- Family-centered, warm + expressive
- Regional differences (São Paulo ≠ Rio ≠ Northeast)
- Coffee culture

**Portugal**:
- Football (Cristiano Ronaldo, Liga Portugal)
- Fado music
- Atlantic + Mediterranean influences
- Tile + tile-pattern aesthetics

## SEO + GEO specifics

- **Brazil**: Google.com.br dominant (~95%); also Bing for older audience
- **Portugal**: Google.pt dominant (~95%)
- Mobile-first across both
- AI search adoption: Brazil ahead, Portugal catching up

## Search behavior

**pt-BR**:
- "Melhor" (best), "Como" (how)
- "Próximo a mim" (near me)
- WhatsApp-style abbreviations leak into queries
- Voice search growing

**pt-PT**:
- "O melhor" (the best) — definite article more common
- More formal natural-language queries
- Less abbreviation than pt-BR

## Regulatory + compliance

**Brazil**:
- ANATEL — telecom
- ANVISA — health
- CVM — financial markets
- LGPD — data protection
- PROCON — consumer protection (advertising)

**Portugal**:
- CNPD — GDPR enforcement
- DECO — consumer protection
- INFARMED — health
- CMVM — financial

## Tone calibration

**pt-BR**:
- Warm, friendly, accessible
- Informal "você" default
- Emojis acceptable in lifestyle/B2C content (not B2B)
- Direct + enthusiastic

**pt-PT**:
- More formal than Brazil
- Subtle humor
- "Bom dia" / "boa tarde" greetings standard
- Less expressive than BR

## Time zones

- Brazil: BRT (UTC-3), Amazon Time (UTC-4)
- Portugal: WET (UTC+0), WEST (UTC+1) summer
- Cape Verde, Angola, Mozambique vary

Publish: 9 AM local for primary market.
