## Language characteristics

**Hong Kong is bilingual**:
- Written Chinese: **Traditional characters** (same as Taiwan)
- Spoken: **Cantonese** (Yue dialect) — different from Mandarin
- English: official language; used in business + government

**Written Cantonese** ≠ written Mandarin:
- 嘅 (Cantonese "of") vs 的 (Mandarin)
- 唔 (Cantonese "not") vs 不 (Mandarin)
- 喺 (Cantonese "at") vs 在 (Mandarin)
- 啦 (Cantonese particle) vs 了/吧 (Mandarin)

**Decision for SEO**: use formal written Chinese (Standard Written Chinese, similar to Taiwan Traditional) for most content. Use Cantonese only in lifestyle/casual B2C.

## Market characteristics

- 7.5M people; very high purchasing power per capita
- World's most expensive real estate market
- Financial services hub
- E-commerce sophisticated; mobile traffic ~70%
- Bilingual sites common

## Cultural references

- Holidays: Lunar New Year (春節), Chinese New Year, Buddha's Birthday, National Day (Oct 1), Christmas
- Cantopop + HK film culture (declining but recognizable)
- Dim sum + yum cha culture
- Banking + finance heritage
- 茶餐廳 (tea restaurants)

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.com.hk dominant (~95%) — unlike mainland
- English-language content also indexed (Hong Kong is bilingual)
- Mobile-first
- AI search adoption fast (international hub)

## Search behavior

- Mixed Chinese + English queries common ("WhatsApp 替代")
- Long-tail Cantonese queries in lifestyle
- Brand searches in English (Apple, Starbucks)
- "邊個" (which one in Cantonese) for comparison searches

## Tone calibration

- More formal than mainland (similar to Taiwan)
- Bilingual sophistication expected
- English brand names kept in English (don't translate "Starbucks" to 星巴克 for HK)
- Younger audiences: more code-switching
- B2B finance: very formal + precise

## Distinctive features

- **Code-switching**: English words integrated into Chinese sentences naturally
- **Cantonese particles**: 啦, 喎, 咩 (use sparingly in formal content)
- **MTR (subway) references**: ubiquitous geographic anchor
- **HK-specific brands**: Watsons, Yata, Citysuper, Pizza Express (not Pizza Hut as in some markets)

## Vocabulary differences vs Mainland China

| Concept | HK (繁體) | Mainland (简体) |
|---|---|---|
| Subway | 港鐵 (MTR) | 地铁 |
| Mobile phone | 手機 | 手机 |
| Internet | 上網 | 上网 |
| Computer | 電腦 | 电脑 |

Similar to Taiwan (Traditional) but with Cantonese particles in casual.
