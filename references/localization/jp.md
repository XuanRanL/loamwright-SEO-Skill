# Locale: Japan (ja / jp)

Japanese market context.

## Quick reference

| Dimension | Setting |
|---|---|
| Currency | JPY `¥` (`¥1,234` — usually no decimals; comma thousands) |
| Date format | `2026年5月19日` (Japanese era: 令和8年5月19日) or `2026/05/19` |
| Distance | km, m (metric only) |
| Weight | kg, g (metric only) |
| Temperature | °C |
| Voice | 敬語 (keigo, polite) by default; 丁寧語 (teineigo) standard |
| Word count | 30-40% SHORTER than English (Japanese is dense) |

## Language notes

- **Character types**: 漢字 (kanji), ひらがな (hiragana), カタカナ (katakana), Latin. Use kanji for content words, hiragana for grammar, katakana for foreign loanwords + emphasis.
- **Politeness levels**:
  - 敬語 keigo (formal): B2B + corporate
  - 丁寧語 teineigo (polite): standard formal writing
  - くだけた kudaketa (casual): B2C youth
- **Particles**: は, が, を, に, で, etc. — critical for meaning. AI translation often gets these wrong.
- **No spaces between words**: Japanese is written without spaces.
- **Punctuation**: 「」for quotes; 、 for comma; 。 for period.
- **Half-width vs full-width**: full-width for body text; half-width for code/URLs/numbers in lists.

## Market characteristics

- Very high digital adoption (mobile ~75%)
- Long, careful purchase decisions (research-heavy)
- B2B: relationship + trust-building over months
- B2C: high quality expectations, low tolerance for typos
- Smartphone-first browsing dominant

## Cultural references

- Holidays: New Year (Jan 1-3), Golden Week (Apr 29 - May 5), Obon (Aug 13-16), Christmas (commercial)
- Anime / manga as common reference points
- Seasons (四季) very important in lifestyle content
- "Wabi-sabi" aesthetic in design
- Politeness + indirectness valued

## SEO + GEO specifics

- Google.co.jp dominant (~75%)
- Yahoo.co.jp uses Google backend but separate market share (~17%)
- Mobile-first absolute
- AI search adoption lagging vs Western markets but growing

## Search behavior

- Very long-tail, specific queries
- Question-format "とは" (what is) common
- "おすすめ" (recommended) for commercial
- "の比較" (comparison) heavily used
- Voice search via Siri / Google Assistant

## Regulatory + compliance

- PPC — Personal Information Protection Commission (privacy)
- CAA — Consumer Affairs Agency (advertising)
- MHLW — health
- FSA — financial services
- Yakuji Law — restricts medicine + cosmetic claims

**Affiliate disclosure**: "*この記事にはアフィリエイトリンクが含まれています。リンクから購入された場合、当サイトに収益が発生する場合がありますが、追加費用は発生しません。*"

(Stricter than US — must be visible BEFORE the affiliate link, not just at footer.)

## YMYL caution

- Health: Yakuji Law strict on medicine + supplement claims
- Financial: FSA requires disclaimers + warnings
- Legal: only 弁護士 (bengoshi, licensed lawyers) can advise

## Content length norms

Japanese is denser than English. Word counts don't translate directly:

| Content type | English (words) | Japanese (characters 文字) |
|---|---|---|
| News-analysis | 1,000w | 2,000-2,500 字 (~1,000 words equivalent) |
| How-to guide | 2,500w | 5,000-6,000 字 |
| Listicle | 5,000w | 10,000-12,000 字 |
| Pillar page | 6,000w | 12,000-15,000 字 |

## Tone calibration

- Polite + indirect (高文脈 — high context)
- 「ます/です」 form for body text (polite)
- Avoid command imperatives ("Do X" → "Xをすると良い")
- Adjective placement varies (use い-adjectives carefully)
- Senpai-kohai dynamics in B2B content

## Distinctive features

- **No exclamation**: Japanese rarely uses ! (looks aggressive); use sentence structure for emphasis
- **Emoticons**: ( ´ ▽ ` ) common in casual; ASCII faces preferred over Western emoji
- **Bullet points**: less common than in English; use prose
- **Heading style**: 「」or 【】for section headers in some contexts

## Time zones

- JST (UTC+9) — year-round; no DST
