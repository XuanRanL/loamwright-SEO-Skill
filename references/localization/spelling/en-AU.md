# Spelling: en-AU

Australian English. Follows UK spelling with minor Australian vocabulary quirks.

## Spelling: 95% identical to en-UK

Use `references/localization/spelling/en-UK.md` for the master pair list. Same forms:
- colour, organise, customise, realise, analyse, optimise
- centre, metre, litre, defence, honour, favour
- catalogue, dialogue, travelling, tyre, aluminium

## Australian-specific vocabulary

| AU | US/UK equivalent |
|---|---|
| arvo | afternoon |
| brekkie | breakfast |
| servo | service station / petrol station |
| bottle-o | liquor store / off-licence |
| esky | cooler |
| sunnies | sunglasses |
| ute | pickup truck |
| thongs | flip-flops (NOT underwear) |
| chook | chicken (informal) |
| dunny | toilet (very informal) |
| g'day | hello (greeting) |
| fair dinkum | genuine / honest |
| she'll be right | it'll be fine |

For formal business content: minimize Australian slang. For casual/lifestyle content: AU slang adds authenticity.

## Numbers + units

- Currency: `AU$1,234.56` or `$1,234.56` (when context is clear)
- Distance: kilometres (km), metres (m). NOT miles.
- Weight: kilograms (kg). NOT pounds.
- Temperature: °C
- Date format: `19 May 2026` or `19/05/2026` (DD/MM/YYYY, never US format)
- Phone: `+61 2 1234 5678` or `(02) 1234 5678`

## Punctuation

- Same as UK (comma outside quotes; logical punctuation)
- Em-dashes: zero tolerance per P13

## Tone defaults

- Direct + friendly (more US-like than UK)
- Informal acceptable for B2C
- Self-deprecating humour common
- "Mate" as friendly term (not for formal business)

## Regulatory references

- ACCC (Australian Competition and Consumer Commission) — advertising + disclosure
- TGA (Therapeutic Goods Administration) — health/medical claims
- ASIC — financial services
- ACMA — telecom + media
