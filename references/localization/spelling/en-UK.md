## Punctuation

- Comma OUTSIDE quote marks: `"hello", she said` (UK convention; "logical punctuation")
- Oxford comma: less common; use only for clarity
- Em-dashes: zero tolerance per P13
- Date format: `19 May 2026` or `19/05/2026` (DD/MM/YYYY)

## Numbers + units

- Currency: `£1,234.56` (comma thousand, dot decimal)
- Mixed imperial + metric:
  - Distance: miles + kilometres
  - Weight: pounds + kilograms (groceries); stone for body weight
  - Temperature: °C (not °F)
- Phone: `+44 20 7946 0958` or `020 7946 0958`

## Idioms (UK-natural)

- "got the sack" (got fired)
- "spanner in the works" (problem)
- "let the side down" (US: "drop the ball")
- "Bob's your uncle" (and there you go)
- "taking the mickey" (joking)
- "queue" (US: "line")
- "rubbish" (as adjective: terrible)

## Tone defaults

- Restrained / understated
- Self-deprecating humour OK
- "Cheers" as informal sign-off
- Less direct than US tone ("would you mind..." vs "please")

## Regulatory references

- ASA (Advertising Standards Authority) — disclosure requirements
- MHRA — health/medical
- Ofcom — telecom + broadcasting
- FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) — financial
