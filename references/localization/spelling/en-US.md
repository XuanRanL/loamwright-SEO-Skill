## Punctuation

- Comma INSIDE quote marks: `"hello," she said` (US convention)
- Oxford comma: optional but increasingly common; consistent within article
- Em-dashes (—): zero tolerance per P13; use commas, colons, or hyphens
- Date format: `May 19, 2026` or `5/19/2026` (NOT `19/5/2026`)

## Numbers + units

- Currency: `$1,234.56` (comma thousand, dot decimal)
- Imperial units default: miles, pounds, feet, inches, °F
- Metric secondary (or omit for purely-US content)
- Phone: `(555) 123-4567` or `555-123-4567`

## Idioms (US-natural)

- "drop the ball" (fail at responsibility)
- "step up to the plate" (take responsibility)
- "Monday morning quarterback" (after-the-fact critic)
- "homer" (home run; success)
- "touch base" (check in briefly)
- "ballpark figure" (rough estimate)
- "left field" (unexpected)

## Tone defaults

- Direct, action-oriented
- Optimistic ("It can be done")
- First-name informal (after first reference)
- "y'all" acceptable in casual content (not formal)

## Regulatory references

- FTC (Federal Trade Commission) — affiliate disclosure
- FDA (Food & Drug Administration) — health claims
- FCC — telecom
- SEC — financial

For affiliate content: "As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases." or similar must appear at/near top of article.
