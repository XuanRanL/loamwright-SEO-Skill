# 12 Content Angles (with title formulas + when-to-use)

> Used by `topic-angle-selector`. Every article picks 1 primary angle. Angles ≠ formats: an angle is the *narrative stance*; a format is the *structure*. A "listicle" format can have a "How-to" or "Comparison" or "Trends" angle.

> **Two-field title note (2026-06-16 title-optimization overhaul):** The title formulas in each angle below describe the **h1 / display title**. From each, derive a short **`seo_title`** (51–60 chars, primary keyword front-loaded, **no year-in-parentheses**) and keep the **entity + any number shared** between the two so Google preserves them and the rewrite risk drops. Power words and digits are **no longer mandatory** — they are optional, register-gated signals. See `docs/title-optimization-plan-2026-06-16.md` for the full rationale.

## 1. How-to / Methodology

**Stance**: Teaching a process
**When to use**: User searches "how to X", "X step-by-step", "X for beginners"
**Best paired with**: how-to-guide, level-guide, tutorial formats
**Title formulas**:
- "How to {action} in {time-frame}: {Power Word} Guide"
- "{Number} Steps to {outcome} (Tested in {N} {cases})"
- "{Action verb}ing {subject}: Step-by-Step for {persona}"

**Register fit**: Broad — fine for all registers including `dtc_celebration`. For `dtc_grief`, only in a gentle, non-commercial framing (hushed, reassurance-forward; never "ultimate", never urgency).

**Avoid**: Vague hooks ("Have you ever wondered..."), "ultimate guide" overuse

## 2. Listicle / Numbered

**Stance**: Authoritative curation with ranking
**When to use**: User searches "best X", "top X", "X for Y"
**Best paired with**: listicle, roundup, shortlist-validation formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{Number} Best {category} for {use case} ({year})" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — parentheses are rewrite-bait)
- "{Number} {Authority Word} {category} That {outcome}"
- "Top {number} {category}: {Power Word} Picks for {year}" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — parentheses are rewrite-bait)

⚠ **AI-tell caution**: a bare `{N} Best {X} ({year})` with no thesis/finding is an AI-tell / SERP-clone pattern — Stage 3.5 rejects it. Lead with the article's actual finding (the decision axis tested), not just the category + year.

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `ecommerce`, `default` — NOT `dtc_grief`.

**Avoid**: Round numbers when 2-digit ones available ("17" > "20"); empty intensifiers

---

## 3. Mistakes / Pitfalls

**Stance**: Cautionary, learn-from-others
**When to use**: User searches "X mistakes", "X problems", "X errors"
**Best paired with**: problem-solution, listicle, troubleshooting formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{Number} {category} Mistakes That {negative outcome}"
- "Why {your X is failing}: {Number} Hidden Issues"
- "{Number} Mistakes Costing You {specific metric} (And How to Fix Them)"

**Register fit**: Broad (problem-solution family) — fine for all registers including `dtc_celebration`. For `dtc_grief`, only in a gentle, non-commercial framing (never "mistakes you're making", no blame, no fear-mongering).

**Avoid**: Fear-mongering without solution; pointing fingers at competitors by name

---

## 4. Cost / Pricing / ROI

**Stance**: Transparent financial analysis
**When to use**: User searches "X cost", "X price", "X ROI", "is X worth it"
**Best paired with**: comparison, case-study, data-research formats
**Title formulas**:
- "What Does {X} Really Cost in {year}? ({Number} Hidden Fees)"
- "{X} ROI: Real {category} Spend $Y to Get $Z"
- "{X} Pricing Breakdown: {Number} Plans Compared"

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `ecommerce`, `default` — NOT `dtc_grief`.

**Avoid**: Affiliate-only motivation; not disclosing financial relationships

---

## 5. Comparison / Versus

**Stance**: Side-by-side analysis
**When to use**: User searches "X vs Y", "X or Y", "X alternatives"
**Best paired with**: comparison, shortlist-validation formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{X} vs {Y}: {Number} {Authority Word} Differences in {year}" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — parentheses/year suffixes are rewrite-bait)
- "{X} or {Y}? Tested in {N} {cases}"
- "{X} vs {Y} vs {Z}: Complete Comparison for {persona}"

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `ecommerce`, `default` — NOT `dtc_grief`.

**Avoid**: Advocacy (always present balanced view); fabricated feature comparisons

---

## 6. Case Study / Results

**Stance**: First-hand validation
**When to use**: User searches "X case study", "how {company} achieved Y"
**Best paired with**: case-study, data-research formats
**Title formulas**:
- "How {company} Achieved {specific result} with {method} (Case Study)"
- "{Specific outcome}: A {Number}-Day {process} Test"
- "{Method} Results: We Tested It on {N} {samples}"

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `default`.

**Avoid**: Generic case studies without specific numbers; fabricated client names

---

## 7. Myths / Truths

**Stance**: Mythbusting, correcting misconceptions
**When to use**: User searches "X myths", "is X true", "X facts"
**Best paired with**: definition, problem-solution, listicle formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{Number} {category} Myths Debunked by {authority/data}"
- "Is {claim about X} True? {Authority Word} Answer for {year}" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — year suffixes are rewrite-bait)
- "Why {common belief about X} Is Wrong (And What's Actually True)"

**Register fit**: Broad — fine for all registers including `dtc_celebration`. For `dtc_grief`, only in a gentle, non-commercial framing (correct misconceptions reassuringly; never "debunk" tone, no superlatives).

**Avoid**: Debunking strawmen; "myths" that no one actually believes

---

## 8. Trends / Forecast

**Stance**: Forward-looking analysis
**When to use**: User searches "X trends", "X 2026", "future of X"
**Best paired with**: news-analysis, data-research, opinion formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{Number} {category} Trends Reshaping {industry} in {year}"
- "The {year} {industry} Outlook: {Number} Shifts to Watch"
- "Why {prediction about X} Will Define {year}"

> ⚠ The year is core to this angle's intent, so it may stay in the seo_title here (it isn't a parenthetical decoration) — but front-load the keyword and keep the same year/number in the h1.

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `default`.

**Avoid**: Crystal-ball claims; trends without data; recycling last year's predictions

---

## 9. Buyer's Guide / Checklist

**Stance**: Decision framework
**When to use**: User searches "X buying guide", "how to choose X", "X checklist"
**Best paired with**: buyers-guide, checklist, comparison formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{X} Buying Guide for {year}: {Number} Factors That Matter" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — year suffixes are rewrite-bait)
- "How to Choose the Right {X} ({Power Word} Checklist)"
- "The {persona}'s Guide to Buying {category}: {Number}-Point Checklist"

**Register fit**: `b2b_technical`, `b2b_procurement`, `ecommerce`, `default` — NOT `dtc_grief`.

**Avoid**: Generic "things to consider"; not weighting factors

---

## 10. Problem-Solution

**Stance**: Diagnostic + remedy
**When to use**: User searches "X problems", "fix X", "X not working"
**Best paired with**: problem-solution, troubleshooting formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{Number} Common {X} Problems (And How to Solve Them in {year})" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — year suffixes are rewrite-bait)
- "Why {X} Isn't {working/converting/ranking}: {Number} Causes + Fixes"
- "Troubleshooting {X}: Solutions for {Number} Common Issues"

**Register fit**: Broad — fine for all registers including `dtc_celebration`. For `dtc_grief`, only in a gentle, non-commercial framing (frame the "problem" as a reader's situation, reassurance-forward; never blame, urgency, or hard-sell fixes).

**Avoid**: Self-promotional fixes; selling a product as the only solution

---

## 11. Localized / Regional

**Stance**: Geographically-specific guidance
**When to use**: User searches "X in {city}", "X for {country/region}"
**Best paired with**: buyers-guide, listicle, level-guide formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{X} in {city/region}: {Number} {category} for {persona}"
- "Best {X} for {country} in {year}: {Authority Word} Picks" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — year suffixes are rewrite-bait)
- "{X} Guide for {region}: {Specific local consideration}"

**Register fit**: Any register matching the underlying business (inherit the project's voice — `b2b_*`, `dtc_*`, `ecommerce`, or `default`). If the business is `dtc_grief`, follow that profile (no "Best/Ranked", gentle framing).

**Avoid**: Stale regional info; copy-paste between regions without genuine localization

---

## 12. Persona / Targeted

**Stance**: Tailored to specific audience segment
**When to use**: User searches "X for {beginners/experts/professionals}", segmented intent
**Best paired with**: level-guide, buyers-guide, how-to-guide formats
**Title formulas**:
- "{X} for {persona}: {Power Word} Guide ({year})" (⚠ keep the year in the h1, not the seo_title — parentheses are rewrite-bait)
- "{Number} {category} Every {persona} Should Know"
- "The {persona}'s Complete Guide to {X}"

**Register fit**: Broad — fine for all registers including `dtc_celebration`. For `dtc_grief`, only in a gentle, non-commercial framing (persona = a reader's situation, not a market segment; no superlatives or hard-sell).

**Avoid**: Stereotyping the persona; using "for dummies" or condescending labels

## Selection decision tree

```
What's the search intent?
  → "how to X"           → angle = how-to
  → "best X" / "top X"   → angle = listicle
  → "X mistakes"          → angle = mistakes
  → "X vs Y"             → angle = comparison
  → "X case study"       → angle = case-study
  → "X myths"             → angle = myths
  → "X trends 2026"       → angle = trends
  → "X buying guide"      → angle = buyers-guide
  → "X problems"          → angle = problem-solution
  → "X cost"             → angle = cost-roi
  → "X in {place}"        → angle = localized
  → "X for {persona}"     → angle = persona-targeted

If no clear pattern, default to listicle for commercial OR how-to for informational.
```

## Distribution requirement (per `topic-angle-selector` skill)

When generating 6 candidates, distribute across ≥3 different angles. Don't generate 6 listicles.

Recommended distribution per 6 candidates:
- 2 from intent-matching angle
- 2 from adjacent angles (e.g., for listicle intent: 1 comparison, 1 buyers-guide)
- 2 wild-card (myths, trends, case-study)

This ensures the topic-angle-selector has real choice, not just rephrasings.
