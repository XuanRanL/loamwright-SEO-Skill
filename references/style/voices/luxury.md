# Voice: Luxury

Refined, restrained, premium. Less is more. Quality over quantity. Reader is treated as discerning — already knows the basics. Tone evokes craftsmanship + heritage + curation. Avoids hype + bargain language.

## Best for

- Luxury retail (watches, jewelry, fashion)
- High-end hospitality
- Premium real estate
- Fine dining content
- Premium spirits + wine
- High-end automotive
- Custom craftsmanship (bespoke tailoring, custom audio)
- Heritage brands
- Curated travel
- Limited-edition product launches

## Hard rules

1. **Less is more** — shorter sentences, fewer adjectives, white space
2. **Specific over superlative** — name the craftsman, not "world-class craftsmen"
3. **Heritage matters** — when was this established? by whom?
4. **No sale language** — never "deal", "discount", "bargain", "value"
5. **Avoid hype words** — no "stunning", "amazing", "incredible"
6. **Materiality** — describe materials, processes, provenance
7. **Restraint in description** — let object speak for itself

## Voice characteristics

| Trait | Setting |
|---|---|
| Contractions | Sparing — formal-leaning |
| First person | Rare; brand voice is the narrator |
| Direct address | Selective — when invited |
| Sentence length | Medium (15-25w); short for emphasis |
| Burstiness | Moderate (SD 5-7) |
| Paragraph length | Short to medium (50-100w) — white space matters |
| Tone | Composed, observant, knowing |

## Vocabulary preferences

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Hand-finished by..." | "Made by skilled craftsmen" (vague) |
| "Established 1847..." | "Founded by industry leaders" |
| "Vacheron Constantin..." | "A famous brand..." |
| "Sourced from..." | "High-quality materials..." |
| "Available in three configurations" | "Many options to choose from" |
| "By appointment" | "Easy to schedule" |
| "Considered detail" | "Stunning attention to detail" |

## Required moves

### Open with object, not pitch

✗ "Discover our amazing new luxury watch collection!"
✓ "The Patek Philippe 5740/1G-001 is a perpetual calendar, automatic, with a gold case finished by hand in their Plan-les-Ouates workshop. Production is limited to approximately 200 pieces per year."

### Specify materiality

What is it made of? Where does the material come from? How is it processed?

- "Vegetable-tanned saddle leather from Tärnsjö Garveri (Sweden)"
- "100% Mongolian cashmere, brushed and twisted by hand"
- "18-karat gold inlaid with mother-of-pearl from the Philippines"

### Heritage and provenance

When relevant, mention:
- Year established
- Craftsman or designer name
- Generations of family ownership
- Material source

### Restraint in praise

Trust the reader to recognize quality. Don't insist.

✗ "This stunning piece is absolutely breathtaking."
✓ "Each piece is signed by the maker."

### Numbers matter (specifically)

Production numbers, years, weights, hours of labor:
- "Limited to 50 pieces"
- "12 weeks to produce"
- "Hand-cut by 7 craftspeople"

## Banned in luxury voice

- "Stunning" / "Amazing" / "Incredible" (hyperbole)
- "Affordable luxury" (contradiction)
- "Deal" / "Discount" / "Sale" (price-focused language)
- "Anyone can afford..." (luxury rejects democratization framing)
- "The best of the best" (vague superlative)
- "Game-changing" / "Revolutionary" (tech-bro language)
- Exclamation marks (more than 1-2 per piece)
- Emojis (with rare exceptions)
- "FOMO" / "Limited time" (urgency language)

## Sample passage

### Bad (luxury done wrong — falls into hype)
> ABSOLUTELY STUNNING new watches just dropped!!! 🔥 You won't BELIEVE the prices on these BEAUTIES! Limited time DEAL — get yours NOW before they're GONE! #blessed #worthit

### Good (luxury voice + general purpose)
> The Lange 1 has remained essentially unchanged since A. Lange & Söhne reintroduced it in 1994. The off-center main dial, the outsized date in its own aperture, the small seconds at seven o'clock — Walter Lange and his team designed these proportions with intention. They have not been revised.
>
> What has changed is the movement. The Caliber L121.1, introduced in 2015, replaced the original L901.0. The improvements are visible only when the caseback is removed: 12.1mm thick, 30.6mm diameter, finished in German silver with hand-applied anglage on each bridge.
>
> Production is limited. Glashütte employs approximately 700 people across three workshops. Each Lange 1 takes 90-100 hours of finishing. Annual output is fewer than 5,000 pieces across all references.
>
> The current pink gold version retails at €52,000. The waiting list is approximately 18 months.

## Combining with purposes

| Voice + purpose | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `luxury + general` | Premium brand blog content |
| `luxury + essay` | Heritage / craftsmanship stories |
| `luxury + email` | High-end client newsletters |
| `luxury + marketing` | Premium product launches |
| `luxury + technical` | High-end mechanical / horological |

## When NOT to use

- Mass-market B2C
- Discount / value brands
- SaaS / tech (use professional or technical)
- Lifestyle blogging (use casual or storyteller)
- Educational content (use teacher)
- News reporting (use journalist)
- Personal stories (use storyteller)

## Soul Injection compatibility

Selective — luxury voice values restraint:

- ⚠ #1 Strong opinions (use sparingly; restraint preferred)
- ✗ #2 Honest uncertainty (luxury communicates confidence)
- ✓ #3 Specific sensory detail (essential — material + craft)
- ✗ #4 Shared experience callback (breaks elevated tone)
- ✓ #5 Allow tangents (controlled — heritage anecdotes)
- ⚠ #6 Dramatic paragraph variation (restrained variation)
- ✗ #7 Imperfect opening (luxury opens with object)
- ✓ #8 Break parallel structure (subtly)
- ✓ #9 Callbacks (to heritage)
- ✗ #10 Self-correct (luxury maintains composure)
- ✓ #11 End without wrap-up (let the object speak)
