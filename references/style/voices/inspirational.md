# Voice: Inspirational

Motivational without being cringe. Aspiration backed by specific data + steps. Reader is treated as capable but currently undersupplied with information. NOT motivational-poster style — more "considered coach giving you what you need to act."

## Best for

- Fitness + wellness content
- Personal growth / self-improvement
- Career advancement
- Leadership content
- Entrepreneurship
- Financial literacy (with caveats)
- Education / learning content
- Sports + athletic training
- Habit formation content
- Productivity content

## Hard rules

1. **Aspiration + concrete steps** — never just aspiration; always pair with what-to-do
2. **Data-backed motivation** — show why the change matters with numbers
3. **No empty positivity** — "you can do it!" is cringe; "you can do it in 12 weeks because..." is real
4. **Honest about difficulty** — naming the hard part EARNS trust
5. **Specific milestones** — not "improve" but "improve by X% in Y weeks"
6. **Stories of transformation** — real, not invented
7. **Avoid toxic positivity** — acknowledge setbacks, not just wins

## Voice characteristics

| Trait | Setting |
|---|---|
| Contractions | Yes (warmth) |
| First person | Selective (when sharing experience) |
| Direct address | Frequent — speaking TO the reader |
| Sentence length | Mixed (8-30 words) |
| Burstiness | Moderate-high (SD 6-8) |
| Paragraph length | Short to medium (40-80w) |
| Tone | Warm, capable, refusing the easy lie |

## Vocabulary preferences

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "You can do this in 12 weeks if..." | "You can do anything!" |
| "Most people who try X give up at week 3 because..." | "Don't give up!" |
| "The 5% who succeed have one thing in common: Y" | "Believe in yourself!" |
| "Here's what no one tells you..." | "It's a journey!" |
| "The hardest part is hour 4 of day 2" | "Stay consistent!" |
| "Three small wins per week beats one big push" | "Crush your goals!" |
| "You'll want to quit on day 8" | "Push through!" |

## Required moves

### Aspiration + specific path

✗ "You can change your life."
✓ "You can shift your weekly habits in 12 weeks. The first 3 weeks are the hardest. Here's what they look like."

### Acknowledge difficulty + provide map

Name the hard part. Explain why it's hard. Show how others navigate it.

✗ "Stay positive!"
✓ "Week 3 is when most people stop. The novelty has worn off; the habit hasn't formed; you're tired. This is normal. Here's what gets people through week 3:"

### Use real transformation stories

Not invented "Steve, our customer..." — actual case studies with verifiable details.

### Data behind the motivation

Show the math:
- "73% of those who track daily reach 12 weeks; 12% who don't."
- "The cost of inaction over 5 years is $X."
- "Compound effect: 1% daily = 37x improvement over a year."

### Specific small wins > vague big goals

"Lose 1 lb this week and add 1 strength session" > "Lose 50 lbs!"

## Banned in inspirational voice

- "Anything is possible!" (vague)
- "Believe in yourself" (empty)
- "Just do it" (oversimplified)
- "Crush it" / "Crushing it" (corporate-pep)
- "Slay" / "Slaying" (forced)
- "Unleash your potential" (already in banned-words.md)
- "Your best self" (vague)
- "The grind" (without context)
- "Manifest" (without irony)
- "Vibes only" (without specific outcome)
- "Just trust the process" (cop-out)
- "It is what it is" (gives up)

## Sample passage

### Bad (inspirational done wrong — empty)
> You can do anything you set your mind to! Believe in yourself! Stay positive! Trust the journey! Hustle, grind, slay! Manifest your best life! Anything is possible if you just BELIEVE!

### Good (inspirational voice + general purpose)
> Most people who try to learn a language give up at 60-90 days. Not because they can't — because the early progress slowed.
>
> Weeks 1-4 you feel fast progress: 200 vocab words a week, basic sentences forming. Then weeks 5-12 you plateau. Vocab grows slower. Grammar still trips you. You're not advanced but you've left "beginner." This is the death zone.
>
> Here's what gets people through.
>
> First, redefine "progress." In weeks 5-12 you're not learning new words as fast — you're consolidating the first 800 words. They're moving from "I recognize this" to "I produce this." That's invisible but more valuable than week 2 was.
>
> Second, shift from input (reading, listening) to output (speaking, writing). Even 10 minutes of forcing yourself to use what you know beats 60 minutes of passive content.
>
> Third, find a 30-minute daily ritual with no flexibility. Same time. Same place. Same first action. The decision-making is what kills you, not the work itself. Make the decision once.
>
> By week 13, the people still doing this are roughly 12% of those who started. They're also roughly 95% of people who reach functional fluency in 18 months. Joining that 12% is the whole game.

## Combining with purposes

| Voice + purpose | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `inspirational + general` | Personal growth blog posts |
| `inspirational + essay` | Long-form aspirational essays |
| `inspirational + email` | Coach / mentor newsletter |
| `inspirational + marketing` | Course / coaching sales (high authenticity required) |
| `inspirational + technical` | Skill-acquisition guides |

## When NOT to use

- News reporting (use journalist)
- Technical documentation (use technical)
- Financial advice (use authoritative)
- Medical content (use professional)
- Legal content (use authoritative)
- Comparison articles (use professional or journalist)

## Industry warnings

DON'T use inspirational voice with:
- Quick-money "make $10K in 7 days" claims (compliance + ethics)
- Health claims without credentials (YMYL)
- "Lose weight fast" content (regulated)
- Investment "secrets" (SEC + FCA risk)

USE inspirational voice with:
- Skill development (learning languages, coding, etc.)
- Habit formation (fitness, productivity)
- Career advancement (skill building)
- Leadership development
- Athletic training
- Education

## Soul Injection compatibility

Heavy — inspirational voice REQUIRES soul:

- ✓ #1 Strong opinions (the coach's voice)
- ✓ #2 Honest uncertainty (vulnerability is motivating)
- ✓ #3 Specific sensory detail (real transformations have specifics)
- ✓ #4 Shared experience callback ("you've been there")
- ✓ #5 Allow tangents (related insights)
- ✓ #6 Dramatic paragraph variation
- ✓ #7 Imperfect opening
- ✓ #8 Break parallel structure
- ✓ #9 Callbacks (to earlier moments in the piece)
- ✓ #10 Self-correct (vulnerability)
- ✓ #11 End without wrap-up (open call to action)
