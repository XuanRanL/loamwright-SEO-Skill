# Purpose: Essay

Long-form argument-driven writing. Structured but not formulaic. Reader is reading for ideas, not just information. Examples: opinion pieces, industry analysis, personal-experience deep dives, op-eds.

## Layer rules (on top of voice)

- **No contractions** (unless `casual` voice; then sparing contractions OK)
- **Formal headings** that label sections, not questions
- **Structured arguments** with explicit thesis statement up top
- **Evidence triple required** for every numeric claim
- **Footnotes or References section** for citations (APA 7)

## Structural defaults

```
Opening (~150-300w):
  - Hook (concrete anecdote, surprising data, or sharp claim)
  - Stake (why this matters)
  - Thesis (the claim being argued)

Body (60-80% of total):
  Section 1: Establish the problem / context
  Section 2: First argument or evidence cluster
  Section 3: Second argument or evidence cluster
  Section 4: Counter-arguments (steel-manned, not strawmanned)
  Section 5: Synthesis / what this means

Closing (~200-400w):
  - Restate thesis with new framing
  - Implications for reader
  - Open question or next step (NOT generic positive)
```

Length: 1500-4000+ words (essays earn their length by depth, not by repetition).

## Required moves

1. **State the thesis explicitly within first 200 words**
   - Not "I'm going to explore..." (P29 violation)
   - Yes "The claim of this piece is that X." or "After 6 months testing Y, I think..."

2. **Address strongest counter-argument**
   - Steel-man the opposing view before refuting it
   - Acknowledge what's right about it

3. **Cite original sources, not summaries**
   - Crossref-verified for academic
   - Direct publisher URLs for journalism
   - DOIs > URLs for academic

4. **End without wrapping up genuinely**
   - Soul Injection #11: it's OK to leave threads
   - But conclude with a clear "so what?"

## Banned in essay purpose

- "In this essay, we will explore..." (P29 + P32)
- "In conclusion..." (lazy synthesis marker)
- Generic positive endings ("The future is bright for X" — P24)
- Three-sentence paragraphs in a row (signal of thin thought)
- Listicle structure (essays argue; they don't enumerate)
- Marketing language (P4)

## What to keep from voice layer

All voice rules still apply. The purpose layer adds formality + argument structure on top.

## Sample openings

### Bad (essay purpose done wrong)
> In this essay, we will explore the multifaceted implications of remote work in today's rapidly evolving digital landscape. We will examine how this transformative shift has impacted productivity, collaboration, and organizational culture.

### Good (professional voice + essay purpose)
> After two years running a fully remote engineering team, I'm convinced that "remote vs office" is the wrong question. The real divide is between teams designed for asynchronous work and teams pretending Zoom is enough.
>
> Of the 23 remote engineering managers I interviewed for this piece, 19 said the same thing: hybrid is harder than either pure remote or pure office. The hybrid teams that work make explicit what async teams figure out naturally — and what office teams never had to think about. This essay is about what those teams do differently.

## Common formats that fit essay purpose

- Industry analysis (4000+w)
- Postmortems with broader lessons (2500-4000w)
- "What I learned from X" deep dives (2000-3500w)
- Op-eds and position pieces (1500-2500w)
- "The case for/against X" arguments (2000-4000w)

## Combining with voice

| Voice + essay | Typical use |
|---|---|
| `professional + essay` | Industry analysis, business writing |
| `technical + essay` | Architecture critiques, engineering opinion |
| `blunt + essay` | Polemics, takedowns, contrarian takes |
| `warm + essay` | Personal experience essays |
| `casual + essay` | Substack-style personal essays (least formal) |

## Word budget allocation

| Section | % of total |
|---|---|
| Hook + stake + thesis | 8-15% |
| Context / problem | 15-20% |
| Argument 1 | 20-25% |
| Argument 2 (or counter) | 20-25% |
| Synthesis | 15-20% |
| Closing | 8-12% |
